Case Study · 01

SmartHealth — telemedicine for every pair of hands.

Role  Lead UX Designer
Platform  Tablet · Smart display
Year  2024

The problem

SmartHealth's existing tablet UX relied on dense button grids and tiny on-screen controls. Older patients, patients with limited mobility, and anyone holding a child mid-call were dropping out before the consult even started. Support call volume for "I can't find the mute button" was the loudest signal of a deeper issue.

The approach

I rebuilt the consult around a hands-optional model. Voice became a first-class input, not a buried accessibility setting. Every voice action had a visual twin — a 44pt target, a clear label, and a confirmation state — so patients could choose how to navigate without ever feeling the system assumed a mode. The flow was validated with 11 patients across three age cohorts and iterated twice before launch.

The outcome

A quieter, calmer consult that passed WCAG 2.1 AA across contrast, focus, and motion, shipped as a reusable pattern across the broader telemedicine suite.

Case Study · 02

Coffee.co — a mobile shop that behaves like a flagship.

Role  Product Designer
Platform  iOS · Android
Year  2023

The problem

Coffee.co's mobile store was a scaled-down desktop. Product pages fought for space, checkout took seven taps, and components had drifted so far from each other that the site felt stitched together from three different stores. Conversion lagged desktop by more than half.

The approach

I started by pulling the system apart — colors, type, spacing, buttons — and rebuilding it as a single token-first library. Every screen was redesigned against that library, with GA4 funnels and Hotjar sessions driving the sequence of experiments. The product detail page, cart, and checkout each shipped as their own A/B against the previous build.

The outcome

A design system that paid for itself in the first quarter, and a mobile funnel that finally pulled its weight against desktop.
